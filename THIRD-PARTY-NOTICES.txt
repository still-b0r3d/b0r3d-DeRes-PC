b0r3d DeRes includes the following third-party software.

fdlibm's cube root, power, sine, cosine and exponential routines
http://www.netlib.org/fdlibm
b0r3d DeRes works out cube roots, powers, sines, cosines and exponentials the way the
website's engine does in Node, so its pictures come out the same as img.b0r3d.org's. Its
copies of these routines were translated into C# from the V8 JavaScript engine's versions
of them (src/base/ieee754.cc, https://github.com/v8/v8), which are adapted from fdlibm and
FreeBSD's msun.

Rust's maths library (libm), as Rust 1.98.1 builds it for WebAssembly
https://github.com/rust-lang/compiler-builtins (libm)
The Signal works out its sines, cosines, tangents, powers and exponentials, and its
logarithms, with copies of the routines Rust's libm has (src/Engine/RustMath.cs), so they
come out as the website's WebAssembly works them out. libm is available under the MIT
licence; its routines come from musl and FreeBSD's msun.
