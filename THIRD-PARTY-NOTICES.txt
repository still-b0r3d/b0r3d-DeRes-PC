fdlibm's cube root, power, sine, cosine and exponential routines
http://www.netlib.org/fdlibm
b0r3d DeRes works out cube roots, powers, sines, cosines and exponentials the way the
website's engine does in Node, so its pictures come out the same as img.b0r3d.org's. Its
copies of these routines were translated into C# from the V8 JavaScript engine's versions
of them (src/base/ieee754.cc, https://github.com/v8/v8), which are adapted from fdlibm and
FreeBSD's msun.

Unicode's case mappings (Unicode 17.0)
https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/
The EXIF tools turn letters into capitals and small letters as JavaScript does, so they
read tags as img.b0r3d.org reads them. Their tables (src/Exif/JsCase.Data.cs) were made
from JavaScript's own results in Node 24 (V8, with ICU 78.3), which follow the case
mappings and properties of the Unicode Character Database (UnicodeData.txt,
SpecialCasing.txt and DerivedCoreProperties.txt).
